## How to use this pack

Every section below is written to be copy-pasted directly into the corresponding field on the MahaRERA agent registration portal (maharera.maharashtra.gov.in). Replace the bracketed placeholders — e.g. `[Proprietor Full Name]`, `[Your Latur address]` — with your actual details before submitting. Where the portal field is shorter than the drafted paragraph, use the trimmed "short version" provided.

---

## ⚠️ Before you apply — the Agent Competency Exam

> Under MahaRERA Order No. 1A/2023 and subsequent circulars, every individual real estate agent (and at least one designated person of every firm) must clear the **MahaRERA Agent Competency Examination** conducted by IBBI/MahaRERA *before* registration or renewal can be granted. Register and clear this exam on the MahaRERA agent-exam portal before submitting your application — the pass certificate is a mandatory upload.
>
> **Confirm the latest fee, syllabus and exam slots on the official MahaRERA website before paying any third party.**

## 02 · Business description

Use the long version in any "About the Business", "Brief Profile" or attached letterhead. Use the short version where character limits apply.

### LONG VERSION (~150 words)

> HousingCorner is a Maharashtra-based real estate channel partner founded with a single conviction: every buyer deserves a transparent, paperwork-clean path to their corner of property. Operating as a sole proprietorship from Latur, HousingCorner connects homebuyers, investors and commercial occupiers with verified residential and commercial inventory exclusively from RERA-registered builders across Maharashtra. The firm combines on-ground site visits and personal advisory with a tech-forward digital platform — a dedicated website, structured listings, WhatsApp-based discovery and CRM-led follow-up — so that every introduction, negotiation and handover is documented, compliant and in the buyer's best interest. HousingCorner does not solicit, advertise or facilitate any unregistered project, and operates strictly within the framework of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and the rules and circulars issued by MahaRERA from time to time.

### SHORT VERSION (~60 words)

> HousingCorner is a Latur-based sole proprietorship operating as a tech-forward real estate channel partner across Maharashtra. We connect buyers and investors with verified residential and commercial properties from RERA-registered builders, combining personal advisory with structured digital workflows. Every transaction is conducted in strict compliance with the RERA Act, 2016 and MahaRERA directions.

### VISION

> To become Maharashtra's most trusted technology-enabled channel partner — the default first call for any buyer or investor seeking a verified, RERA-registered residential or commercial property in the state — by combining old-school local accountability with modern, transparent digital workflows.

### MISSION

## 04 · Services offered

Paste under "Services" / "Nature of Business" / "Scope of Work". Bullets read cleanly when copied.

- **Residential brokerage** — Buyer advisory and property matching for residential apartments, villas and plotted developments in MahaRERA-registered projects.
- **Commercial brokerage** — Sourcing of office spaces, retail units, showrooms, warehouses and mixed-use commercial inventory from RERA-registered commercial projects.
- **Channel partnership** — Curated, compliance-checked introductions between RERA-registered builders/developers and qualified buyers, with end-to-end follow-up till registration.
- **Site visits & disclosure** — Verified site visits, project walkthroughs, payment-plan explainers and possession-timeline disclosures sourced directly from the MahaRERA portal.
- **Loan & documentation support** — Coordinated assistance with home-loan referrals to scheduled banks/NBFCs (HousingCorner does not lend, underwrite or collect EMIs).
- **Resale advisory** — Resale brokerage of units in completed RERA-registered projects, in line with applicable MahaRERA circulars.
- **Digital property discovery** — WhatsApp-led property discovery, listing dashboards and CRM-managed lead servicing for both buyers and partner builders.

## 08 · Code of conduct & compliance commitment

Useful for the "Declaration" / "Compliance Statement" field, and as content on the website / letterhead.

### COMPLIANCE COMMITMENT

> HousingCorner commits to the following standards of conduct in every transaction it facilitates:
>
> 1. Deal exclusively in MahaRERA-registered projects;
> 2. Quote the MahaRERA registration number and project registration number in every advertisement, brochure, website listing and digital communication;
> 3. Provide buyers with accurate, written disclosure of project status, carpet area, amenities, payment plan and possession timeline as published on the MahaRERA portal;
> 4. Never collect any consideration on behalf of a builder outside of the channels and instruments approved by that builder and disclosed to the buyer;
> 5. Maintain proper books of accounts and records of every transaction facilitated, and submit the Half-Yearly Progress Report (Form 6) and any other returns prescribed by MahaRERA on time;
> 6. Cooperate fully with any inquiry, scrutiny or direction issued by the Authority.
