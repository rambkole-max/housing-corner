Questions

What buyers ask about this project

What is the MahaRERA number for {{PROJECT_NAME}}, {{LOCALITY_SHORT}}?

{{RERA_NUMBER}}. Verify the registration, sanctioned plans and declared completion date yourself at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in before you pay anything. Note that the commercial cost sheet we hold prints the number with an "A" prefix — that appears to be a typographical error in the developer's sheet; the brochure and the portal both show "P".

What is the carpet area of a 2 BHK here?

RERA carpet area is 636 to 678 sq ft depending on the unit, with a separate balcony of 140 to 179 sq ft. The 789 to 840 sq ft figure in the developer's cost sheet is carpet plus balcony combined, and pricing is calculated on that combined figure. If another listing quotes you "840 sq ft carpet" for this project, it is quoting carpet plus balcony.

What does a 2 BHK cost, all in?

Agreement cost runs from roughly ₹75.1 lakh to ₹80.0 lakh by unit. Add 7% stamp duty, 5% GST and ₹32,000 registration, and the all-in figure is approximately ₹84.5 lakh to ₹89.9 lakh. Common maintenance of about ₹80,000 covering roughly 24 months is payable separately at possession.

When is possession?

We are not publishing a possession date for this project, because we do not have one we can stand behind. The declared completion date is recorded on the MahaRERA portal against {{RERA_NUMBER}} — that is the date the developer is legally bound to, and it is the only one worth relying on. We will pull it up with you.

How far is it from Kothrud and Sinhgad Road?

The site fronts the Pune–Bangalore Highway. Sinhgad Road is 1.5 km, Kothrud 2 km, Bavdhan 3 km, Kothrud Bus Depot 3 km, Deccan 6 km and Swargate 7 km, per the developer's stated distances.

Are the commercial shops still available, and what do they cost?

Four shops sit on the ground floor with highway frontage, from 230 to 322 sq ft carpet plus a 58 to 81 sq ft loft. We are not publishing shop prices because the rate card we hold expired in September 2025 and contains a pricing error on two of the four units. Ask us and we will confirm the current rate with {{DEVELOPER}} and send it to you in writing.

Can I get a home loan on this project?

Yes. The developer works with a panel of lenders and their loan executives. If you would rather use your own bank, that is your right, but note the developer applies a ₹30,000 administration charge for loans arranged outside their panel. We will coordinate either route and we do not lend, underwrite or collect EMIs ourselves.

Do I pay HousingCorner a fee?

No. We are compensated by the developer once a transaction is registered. You pay exactly what you would pay walking into the developer's office directly.
