Questions

What buyers ask about this project

What is the MahaRERA number for Royal Paradise, Warje?

P52100049617. Verify the registration, sanctioned plans and declared completion date yourself at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in before you pay anything. Note that the commercial cost sheet we hold prints the number with an "A" prefix — that appears to be a typographical error in the developer's sheet; the brochure and the portal both show "P".

What is the carpet area of a 2 BHK here?

RERA carpet area is 636 to 678 sq ft depending on the unit, with a separate balcony of 140 to 179 sq ft. The 789 to 840 sq ft figure in the developer's cost sheet is carpet plus balcony combined, and pricing is calculated on that combined figure. If another listing quotes you "840 sq ft carpet" for this project, it is quoting carpet plus balcony.

What does a 2 BHK cost, all in?

Agreement cost runs from roughly ₹75.1 lakh to ₹80.0 lakh by unit. Add 7% stamp duty, 5% GST and ₹32,000 registration, and the all-in figure is approximately ₹84.5 lakh to ₹89.9 lakh. Common maintenance of about ₹80,000 covering roughly 24 months is payable separately at possession.

When is possession?

June 2027, as recorded against MahaRERA registration P52100049617. You can confirm it yourself by searching that number at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in, where the completion date and the quarterly progress reports are both on the public record. We will walk you through that page on a call if it helps.

How far is it from Kothrud and Sinhgad Road?

The site fronts the Pune–Bangalore Highway. Sinhgad Road is 1.5 km, Kothrud 2 km, Bavdhan 3 km, Kothrud Bus Depot 3 km, Deccan 6 km, Swargate 7 km and Hinjewadi 14 km, per the developer's stated distances.

Are there shops for sale in Warje at Royal Paradise, and what do they cost?

Four shops sit on the ground floor with highway frontage, from 230 to 322 sq ft carpet plus a 58 to 81 sq ft loft. We are not publishing shop prices because the rate card we hold expired in September 2025 and contains a pricing error on two of the four units. Ask us and we will confirm the current rate with Royal Properties and send it to you in writing.

What is the GST and stamp duty on a commercial shop here?

Commercial units attract GST at 12%, against 5% on the residential flats in the same building — that gap surprises a lot of first-time commercial buyers. Stamp duty is 7% on both. Registration on the shops is ₹30,000, legal fees of ₹15,000 are payable at agreement, and the booking amount is ₹2,00,000.

What kind of business suits a shop at Royal Paradise?

The units are sized for a showroom, clinic, pharmacy, salon, cafe or a branch office. Footfall comes from two directions: frontage on the highway service road, and the 56 households on the seven residential floors directly above. Shops 1 and 4 are the larger corner units at 403 sq ft total carpet; Shops 2 and 3 are 288 sq ft.

Can I get a home loan on this project?

Yes. The developer works with a panel of lenders and their loan executives. If you would rather use your own bank, that is your right, but note the developer applies a ₹30,000 administration charge for loans arranged outside their panel. We will coordinate either route and we do not lend, underwrite or collect EMIs ourselves.

Do I pay HousingCorner a fee?

No. We are compensated by the developer once a transaction is registered. You pay exactly what you would pay walking into the developer's office directly.
