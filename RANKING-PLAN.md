# Ranking plan — Warje, Pune

*Competitor research run 12 August 2026 against live Google results, Pune location.*

The goal you set is to be **first for anyone searching for a home or an office in
Warje.** This document answers that honestly: which parts of it are winnable,
which are not, and what to do in what order.

---

## 1 · The honest answer on "first for homes in Warje"

**Not realistically, and chasing it would waste your money.**

Here is what the first page of *2 BHK flats for sale in Warje Pune* looks like
today. Every single result is a national portal:

NoBroker · 99acres · MagicBricks · OLX · Houssed · Housing.com · SquareYards ·
360Realtors

Housing.com alone lists **2,283 two-BHK flats in Warje**. NoBroker lists 68 on
one page. These sites have twenty years of domain authority, millions of pages,
and full-time SEO teams. A three-page site with one listing does not outrank them
on a head term, and no amount of on-page work changes that in the near term.

**The exception is the map pack.** When someone searches *property dealer in
Warje* or *real estate agent near me* from a Pune phone, Google shows three local
business listings above the organic results. Portals cannot occupy those. That is
a winnable "first position" and it costs nothing but a verified address.

## 2 · Where you can genuinely be first

### 2.1 · Commercial in Warje — the open goal

This is the finding that matters most, and it is the opposite of the residential
picture.

| Query | What the SERP looks like |
|---|---|
| *2 BHK flats in Warje* | Housing.com alone has 2,283 listings |
| *commercial shop for sale in Warje* | **Housing.com has 3.** SquareYards has "1+" |

The entire indexed commercial inventory for Warje is a handful of units, and the
listings that do rank are mostly ₹1.4 Cr to ₹19 Cr office floors — a different
buyer entirely from someone wanting a ₹1 Cr shop.

**You have four shops on the highway service road and a dedicated page section for
them.** Almost nobody is competing. Of the ten portals carrying Royal Paradise,
only one mentions the shops exist at all.

Target: *shop for sale in Warje*, *commercial shop Warje Pune*, *showroom for sale
Warje*, *shop for sale on Mumbai Bangalore highway Pune*. This is where your first
real ranking will come from — and commercial buyers are worth more per transaction
than flat buyers.

**The blocker is your own honesty policy.** The page says "price on request"
because the developer's rate card expired in September 2025 and has a transposition
error. Pages without a price rank worse and convert worse. **Get a corrected
commercial rate card from Royal Properties.** That single phone call is worth more
than a month of SEO work.

Read that table again. A ₹14 lakh spread on price. A 292 sq ft spread on size.
"4 floors" versus 7. Three mutually exclusive possession dates on one page.

None of them cite a source. **You have the architect's sanctioned drawing and the
developer's cost sheet**, and your page reproduces both. That is a real, defensible
advantage that no portal can copy, because they scrape rather than verify.

Two of their errors are worth calling out because they cost buyers money:

- **"All Inclusive Price: ₹75.89 L."** It is not all-inclusive. Stamp duty, GST
  and registration add roughly ₹9 lakh. A buyer budgeting from that number arrives
  at registration nine lakh short.
- **Sizes labelled "carpet area"** that are actually carpet plus balcony. Real RERA
  carpet here is 636–678 sq ft, not 811–840.

A new `#figures` section on the project page now lays all of this out side by side
with sources. It is the most linkable content on the site, it directly targets
*Royal Paradise Warje price*, and it demonstrates the entire value proposition in
one screen.

**Realistic outcome:** top three within two to four months of indexing. Beating
Housing.com to position one is plausible but not guaranteed — domain authority is
real. Position one *for the specific question* "what does Royal Paradise actually
cost" is very achievable.

### 2.3 · Questions nobody has answered properly

Portals publish inventory. They do not explain anything. Every one of these is a
real search with weak competition:

- *RERA carpet area vs carpet plus balcony* — you already have the best explanation
  on the internet for this, sitting inside a project page. **Move it to its own
  guide.**
- *stamp duty and GST on a flat in Pune* — 7% + 5%, with a worked example
- *why is GST 12% on shops and 5% on flats*
- *how to read a MahaRERA project extract*
- *what does "all inclusive price" actually include*

These earn links on merit, which is the only durable authority. They also feed AI
answers in Google's overviews, which increasingly matter more than the blue links.

## 3 · Do these in order

**1 · Upload the site and verify Google Search Console.** Nothing is indexed. The
clock has not started. Everything below is theoretical until this is done.

**2 · Google Business Profile.** The map pack is the only "position one" available
to you in the short term. Needs a **street-level address**, which you still do not
have. This is the biggest single blocker in the whole plan and it also blocks the
letterhead, the business cards and the MahaRERA application.

**3 · Get the corrected commercial rate card.** Unlocks the least competitive,
highest-value SERP available to you. One phone call to Royal Properties.

**4 · ~~Get the possession date from the MahaRERA portal.~~ Done — and it is the
strongest thing on the site.** The registry against P52100049617 records:

- Registered **21 February 2023**
- Original completion **29 June 2025**
- **Revised completion 29 June 2027** — a two-year extension

**Not one property portal is showing the revised date.** 99acres, PropTiger and
BuyIndiaHomes all still show June 2025. Housing.com shows June 2026, June 2025 and
"Ready to Move" on the same page. The project page now has a dedicated
`#possession` section carrying both dates, what the revision means for a buyer's
budget, and instructions to verify it in two minutes.

This is a genuine, checkable fact that every competitor has wrong, on the question
buyers care most about. It targets *Royal Paradise Warje possession date* — a query
with real intent and no correct answer currently ranking.

**One task remains for you:** the dates were read from the MahaRERA registry via a
mirror, because the official portal was unreachable from here. Open
P52100049617 on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in yourself, confirm both dates, and
screenshot the certificate. Publishing a date this important on second-hand
sourcing is below the standard the rest of the site holds to.

**5 · Build `/guides/`.** Start with the carpet-area explainer — it is already
written, it just needs to be its own URL that can rank and be linked to.

**6 · Ask the developer for a link.** Royal Properties runs
`@royal_paradise_warje` on Instagram. A link from the developer to their channel
partner's project page is natural, legitimate, and worth more than a hundred
directory submissions.

**7 · Local citations.** IndiaMART, Justdial, Sulekha, Google Business, Bing
Places. Free, tedious, and genuinely how local ranking works in India. Keep the
name, address and phone **byte-identical** everywhere — inconsistency actively
hurts.

**8 · Site photographs.** Dated construction photos you took yourself. No portal
has them, and Google increasingly rewards demonstrable first-hand experience.

## 4 · What not to do

**Do not buy backlinks.** The "500 backlinks for ₹2,000" sellers who will contact
you once the site is live produce links from spam directories. At best worthless,
at worst a manual penalty.

**Do not list this project on the portals to "get exposure."** You would be paying
to strengthen the exact pages that outrank you, and handing them your verified data
to publish under their brand.

**Do not chase *flats in Pune* or *2 BHK in Warje*.** You will spend money and
rank on page four.

**Do not copy portal descriptions.** Half of them are factually wrong, and
duplicated text is worthless anyway.

---

## 5 · How to tell if it is working

Check Search Console monthly, in this order of importance:

1. **Impressions for *Royal Paradise*** — are you being shown at all?
2. **Position for *shop for sale in Warje*** — the winnable commercial term
3. **Google Business Profile views and direction requests** — local intent
4. **WhatsApp enquiries mentioning the website** — the only metric that pays

Ignore total traffic. Fifty visitors who searched *Royal Paradise Warje price* are
worth more than five thousand who searched *Pune property*.

---

## 6 · The honest summary

You will not be first for *homes in Warje*. You can be first for *shops in Warje*,
because almost nobody is competing there. You can be top three for *Royal Paradise
Warje*, because you have documents the portals do not and they visibly contradict
each other. You can be in the map pack for local searches, as soon as you have an
address.

Your advantage is not that your site is better built, though it is. It is that you
can be *correct* in a market where ten well-funded portals are publishing figures
that disagree by ₹14 lakh — and where one of them is calling a number
"all-inclusive" that is ₹9 lakh short.

Being right is a slow strategy. It is also the only one available to you, and it
compounds.
